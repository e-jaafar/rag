# Incident response

## Critical incident acknowledgement
The on-call responder must acknowledge a severity-one production alert within 5 minutes of the page.

## High incident acknowledgement
The on-call responder must acknowledge a severity-two production alert within 20 minutes of the page.

## Customer status updates
During a severity-one outage, the communications lead posts status updates every 30 minutes until service is restored.

## Incident review
The incident commander publishes the post-incident review within 3 business days of recovery.

## Incident ownership
The duty manager assigns an incident commander when an alert is declared a major incident. The incident commander coordinates the response.

## Customer status channels
Public service announcements are posted on the status page. Internal coordination happens in the dedicated incident channel.

## Emergency videoconference
If the incident channel is unavailable, responders join the bridge using the calendar entry called Ops Bridge.
