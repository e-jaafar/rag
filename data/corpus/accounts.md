# Employee access

## Dormant employee accounts
Employee accounts are suspended after 45 consecutive days without a successful sign-in. This policy excludes service identities and contractor accounts.

## Dormant contractor accounts
Contractor accounts are disabled after 14 consecutive days without a successful sign-in. An active sponsor may request reinstatement.

## Emergency access
Security on-call may issue a break-glass token that expires after 30 minutes. Emergency access events are reviewed the next business day.

## Recovery verification
For a staff account recovery, the service desk verifies the employee through a live video call before issuing a recovery link.

## Temporary privileged access
A production administrator's elevation ends automatically after 90 minutes. Renewal requires a new approval from the duty manager.

## Hardware keys
New platform operators receive two hardware security keys. The spare key should be stored separately from the primary key.

## Service identity rotation
Production service-account credentials are rotated every 60 days. The platform team validates the new credential before revoking its predecessor.
