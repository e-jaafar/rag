# Customer support

## Standard response
Customers on the standard support plan receive a first response within 8 business hours for a normal request.

## Premium response
Customers on the premium support plan receive a first response within 2 business hours for a normal request.

## Support exports
The support portal exports closed tickets in CSV format. Attachments are downloaded separately from each ticket.

## Support attachments
An individual upload to a support ticket can be up to 25 MB. Accepted types include PNG, JPG, and PDF.

## Billing dispute
Customers must submit a billing dispute within 30 calendar days of the invoice date. Finance reviews disputes through the support portal.

## Customer languages
The support desk provides written assistance in English and French. German-language tickets are routed to the translation queue.

## Support holiday cover
During a public holiday, critical customer requests are monitored by the duty team. Normal requests resume on the next business day.
